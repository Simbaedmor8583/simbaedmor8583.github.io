🤖 JARVIS - Boost Daily Productivity With Voice Intelligence

Download JARVIS for Windows

🚀 About This Assistant

JARVIS serves as a central hub for your computer tasks. It combines voice recognition with advanced logic to help you work faster. You can talk to your computer to open apps, write documents, or search the web. The software runs locally and connects to secure cloud services when you need extra power.

✨ Key Features

  • Voice Control: Give commands with your microphone to launch programs.
  • Document Workflow: Use AI to draft emails, reports, and office files.
  • Smart Routing: Choose between private local models or fast cloud performance.
  • Browser Tools: Navigate the internet and summarize pages through voice.
  • Automation: Perform repetitive clicks and keystrokes automatically.

💻 System Requirements

Your computer needs to meet these basic standards to run JARVIS smoothly:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • Processor: 2.0 GHz dual-core or better.
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM.
  • Storage: 500 MB of space for initial setup.
  • Microphone: Required for voice input functions.

📥 Installation Guide

Follow these steps to set up the software on your desktop:

  1. Go to the official release page.
  2. Find the section titled "Assets" at the bottom of the newest version.
  3. Click the file ending in .exe to download the installer to your computer.
  4. Locate the file in your Downloads folder and double-click the icon.
  5. Follow the prompts on your screen to complete the installation.
  6. Open the application from your Start Menu after the process ends.

🛠️ Getting Started

Once you open the software, a setup wizard appears. You can select your voice settings and link your preferred AI services here. If you use OpenRouter, simply paste your key into the settings menu to enable cloud features. You can click the microphone icon at any time to start a voice command.

🛡️ Privacy and Safety

Your data stays on your machine whenever possible. Local processing ensures that your voice commands do not leave your computer for private tasks. When you choose to use cloud services, the software sends only the text of your request. It does not record audio for cloud storage.
